Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. The Maggy CLI tool (zero telemetry)

The maggy command-line tool runs entirely locally on your machine. It does not collect, transmit, or share any telemetry, analytics, prompts, code, or session data.

Locally, maggy only reads and writes configuration and cache files under ~/.config/maggy/ and ~/.cache/maggy/, and — when you run maggy setup or maggy uninstall — updates the statusLine entry in ~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/settings.json.

2. Website analytics (Google Analytics & GTM)

This landing page (agy-pet.github.io) uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics (GA4) with Google Consent Mode v2 to understand overall site traffic, such as page views, referrers, approximate region, language, and browser or device type.

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, analytics storage is denied by default and no analytics cookies are set unless you accept via the consent banner. Advertising storage and personalization signals (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) are always denied globally. We also honor the browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.

When analytics storage is granted, Google Analytics may set cookies or use similar browser storage to distinguish visits and sessions. This data is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

We do not sell personal data, run ads, or collect personally identifiable information (such as names or email addresses) on this site.

3. Managing consent & opting out

You can accept, decline, or change your Google Analytics cookie preference at any time:

  • Open to update your choice on this browser (stored locally under maggy.consent).
  • Enable Global Privacy Control (GPC) or a content blocker, or disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • Install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) to operate and secure the service, subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Changes & contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as the project evolves. The latest version is always posted on this page. If you have questions, open an issue on GitHub.
